#+title: Themes and palettes
#+author: Josep Bigorra
#+options: toc:2

Set ~heks/theme~ to any of the names below and the whole configuration follows:
the faces, the window separators, the sizes of prose, and the colours of a
rendered document or a deck. A palette you have not listed here is a
~heks/theme~ that loads with whatever Emacs already had, which is a thing you
can do and probably do not want.

A curated collection of gorgeous, modern, and highly accessible color palettes
with custom tweaks and a custom Heks interpretation and styling, aiming for
harmony and uniformity across the stack.


* The Ef Collection
- *Dark:* ~ef-autumn~, ~ef-bio~, ~ef-dream~
- *Light:* ~ef-day~, ~ef-kassio~, ~ef-melissa-light~, ~ef-cyprus~

* Accessible & Classic

- ~kaolin-dark~
